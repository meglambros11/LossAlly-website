What We Do

Loss Ally helps families navigate the administrative aftermath of a loss. When someone passes away, the executor — usually a grieving family member — faces dozens of phone calls, letters, institutions, and deadlines with no guide and no one to call. Loss Ally handles that work on their behalf.

We work remotely with families anywhere in the US, managing everything from government notifications and financial account closure to digital estate management and record retention — so families can focus on each other instead of the paperwork.
